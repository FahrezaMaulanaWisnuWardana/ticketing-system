# User guide

## Requesters (1 page)

1. Sign in with Google (or the email/password form). The theme toggle (☀/☾) is available here too, before you log in.
2. Click **+ Create** (or press `C`), choose a project and ticket type, type a title and description, press Create. The description is a rich-text editor: use the toolbar for bold, lists, links and code, or paste/drag files into it (images appear inline, other files become links). The dialog closes with `Esc` or **Cancel**, and you land on the confirmation page with the ticket key and SLA target.
3. Open **My requests** to see your tickets and their friendly status: Open / In Progress / Needs your reply / Resolved. Filter by open/resolved or search.
4. Reply on your ticket. If the status is *Needs your reply*, replying resumes work automatically.
5. A resolved ticket can be reopened for 7 days. Internal team notes are never shown to requesters.

The full visual guide is available as [PANDUAN-PENGGUNAAN.html](PANDUAN-PENGGUNAAN.html) and inside the app from the **User guide** link in the sidebar (`/panduan`). Use the header controls to switch light/dark theme and between ID/EN; both preferences are saved to your account. Sidebar links are permission-gated, so you only see what your role can access.

## Agents / leads (2 pages)

**Page 1 — working**
- My Work groups your assigned tickets; overdue first.
- Helpdesk queue shows unassigned requests by urgency (priority, then oldest). Press `A` to assign the first ticket to yourself, or use the row button.
- Select a ticket in the queue, choose a canned reply and send — or write your own in the rich-text editor. You can format text and attach files by pasting or dragging them into the editor. Internal notes use a yellow background and are never emailed to requesters.
- Use `S` to move a ticket, `/` to search, `?` for shortcut help.

**Page 2 — projects**
- Project overview lists members, categories, canned replies and tickets.
- The **Overview** (Ringkasan) page shows metric cards (completed, updated, created, due soon), a status-distribution chart, and recent activity. Switch the period with **Filter** (7, 30, or 90 days).
- Leads manage members, categories and canned replies; they can edit project details.
- Type `@colleague@example.com` in a comment to mention them; they receive an in-app notification.
- The board, backlog, sprints and reports are available from the project page; dev tickets follow the board flow while helpdesk requests follow the queue flow.
